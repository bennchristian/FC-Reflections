# Why I'd say yes to FC again

When the FC Fellowship was first described to me, my initial thoughts were that this is too good to be true. Two months later, I believe that it was in fact too good to be true!

At the time when I first joined, I was in a deep season of transition. I'd just graduated from law school. I was about to finish an internship and start studying for the bar, but after that I had no clear direction to go. At Arise Asia, I met and worked with many lovely people from this organisation, and they shared that this Fellowship was the space for people exactly like me. There would be mentorship, discipleship, community, and most importantly room to grow in innovation and your own personal skills.

At FC, I built a pipeline matching missionary senders to people groups, streamlining a messy dataset to match the needs of various peoples to those with the resources to support them. I also built prototypes for international student ministry and for Christian content creators, with proper frontend and backend work, despite having minimal experience in code. Through it all, the community was really understanding, warm and welcoming, always caring more about us as people than about what we produced.

I remember telling my friend that I didn't think something was technically possible. Then FC gave us the challenge, and more importantly the bandwidth, to do exactly that. Through testing and iteration, I realised it was more than possible; it was very doable. That experience really cemented for me that it's important to just keep trying and not let my own mind get in the way.

In hindsight, the main takeaway for me was growing in confidence, and I saw it when it came to my next step. I would say this Fellowship even divinely helped me land my next appointment! In my cover letter, I offered to explore how technology and AI could support the firm's work beyond legal matters, pointing to the hands-on experience of building with these tools at FC. Before FC, I would not have offered to help with the firm's business operations on top of legal work. With my experience at FC, I figured that even if I don't have all the knowledge and experience, I can learn, and it can be done. I think I always could have, but without FC as a place to try first, I wouldn't have had the confidence to try.

This experience truly shifted my life's trajectory in ways I probably don't even fully recognise yet. If you're reading this and you have the chance to join, I would recommend it in a heartbeat. If it were up to me, I'd stay on as long as I could. Even as this Fellowship comes to a close, I know I'll keep the FC community close to me.

When FC was first described to me, I thought it was too good to be true. Two months later, I can say it was good, and it was true.
