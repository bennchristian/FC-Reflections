## Music & pacing

- **Music:** warm, hopeful acoustic/piano with soft percussion, ~90–110 BPM, royalty-free and cleared for this use.
- **Arc:**
  - soft intro (0)
  - beat drops (0.5)
  - steady (1)
  - thins at "never done anything like that" (2c), back on "But I adapted"
  - swells through the projects (2d–2e)
  - warm and steady (2f–4c)
  - lifts at "shifted my life's trajectory" (5a)
  - resolves on "good, and it was true" (5c)
- **Level:** about −18 to −20 dB under the VO.
- **Pacing:** something changes every 2–4 s. On-screen text stays ≤6 words, except the cover-letter page, where only the highlighted phrase is meant to be read.
- **Recording:**
  - quiet room, mic ~15–20 cm away
  - 2 s of silence first, then one continuous take
  - after a stumble, pause and restart the sentence
  - a breath between rows, slightly slower than feels natural
  - don't say the `{Cxx}` tags
  - send a WAV or a high-quality M4A
